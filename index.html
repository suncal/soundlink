SoundLink

Send a text message from one device to another using only sound. Open this page on two phones, put them near each other, type on one and press Send — the other hears it and shows the text. Nothing goes through any network.

Send

Tip: keep messages short. ~10 characters/second.

Receive

Idle. Press “Start listening,” then Send from the other device.
A tone-based acoustic modem (16-FSK, Goertzel detection, CRC-checked) running entirely in your browser. Short-range, low-bandwidth by nature — built to prove internet-less messaging, not to replace the internet.
